OUR STORY

Learn about the visionaries bridging the gap between rural talent and national esports glory.

From Local Battles to National Arenas

Fire Village Gaming was born from a simple yet powerful observation: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. While major cities hosted massive esports events, incredibly skilled players in rural and semi-urban areas lacked a structured platform to showcase their abilities.

What started with small, local custom room tournaments quickly ignited a spark. Hundreds of teams registered, proving the immense hunger for competitive gaming outside metropolitan hubs.

Today, Fire Village stands as a premier Indian Esports Tournament Platform. Our mission is to democratize esports, providing fair play, high-quality broadcasting, and real rewards for various games like BGMI, Valorant, Free Fire, and more to the undiscovered gaming legends of tomorrow.
